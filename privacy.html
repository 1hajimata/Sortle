Sortle Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Sortle is a daily ranking game that runs as a Discord Activity and Discord app. This policy explains what it collects and why.

What we collect

  • From Discord, when you open Sortle and approve it: your Discord user ID, display name, and avatar. We use the "identify" permission only. We never see your password, email, messages, or friends.
  • The server ID and channel ID where you launch Sortle.
  • Your gameplay: daily scores, guesses (the green/gray result of each check), dates played, and stats derived from them such as averages, streaks and rankings.

How we use it

  • To show live scores, leaderboards, profiles, and rankings to other players in the same server.
  • To post the scoreboard, streak messages and streak reminders in the Discord channel where Sortle was launched.
  • To keep the game working and prevent cheating and abuse.

Sharing

Your name, avatar and scores are visible to other players in your server and, on the global leaderboard, to other Sortle players. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. It is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the app.

Storage and deletion

We keep gameplay data so leaderboards and stats continue to work. Your token is used only during your session and is not stored. To have your data deleted, message the developer on Discord (username: Swervo) with your Discord user ID and we will remove it.

Children

Sortle is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
